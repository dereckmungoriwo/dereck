Chapter A

My Culinary Foundation

This section documents my six years in the culinary industry.

How it started

My interest in culinary arts began with a simple love of food and the satisfaction of making something people enjoy. Cooking at home turned into curiosity about how professional kitchens work, and I decided to turn that passion into a career.

I enrolled in a Diploma in Professional Cookery, where I learned the core of the trade: knife skills, stocks and sauces, pastry and bakery fundamentals, menu planning, and how a professional kitchen runs. I also earned my HACCP and ServSafe food safety certifications, which shaped the standards I still work to.

  • Diploma in Professional Cookery
  • HACCP food safety certification
  • ServSafe food safety certification

Chapter B

Information Technology

The second path: from curiosity about technology to a degree, real client work and a new career.

How it started

Working in busy kitchens taught me to love systems: clear processes, good tools and teams that run smoothly. Alongside that, I became curious about the technology behind the websites and apps I used every day, and I started to teach myself how they are built.

That curiosity grew into a decision to study properly, and I began pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Information Technology.

Academic experience

My degree covered the foundations of the field and let me explore the areas that interest me most.

  • Studied programming fundamentals, web development, databases, networking and systems analysis.
  • Completed practical projects and a final-year project.
  • Areas of interest: front-end development, user experience and cloud technology.
