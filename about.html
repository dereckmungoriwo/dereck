About me

Getting to Know Me

I am a professional with a background in culinary arts and a growing career in Information Technology. My journey has taken me through two different industries, allowing me to develop a diverse set of professional skills, experiences and perspectives.

Having spent six years working in professional kitchens, I developed a strong appreciation for creativity, precision, teamwork and discipline. My passion for technology eventually inspired me to pursue a bachelor's degree in information technology, opening a new chapter in my professional development.

I believe in continuous learning, embracing new challenges and exploring opportunities that allow me to grow both personally and professionally.

01 / My background

Two worlds, one mindset.

Culinary arts

Professional kitchens taught me to work fast, stay organised and care about the details that guests notice. Pastry, in particular, rewarded patience and precision.

Technology

Information Technology gave me a new way to build and solve problems. I enjoy understanding how systems connect, especially networks and the cloud.

02 / My story

How my path took shape.

I did not set out to work in two industries. My first love was food, and the energy of a kitchen drew me in. I loved making something from nothing and seeing people enjoy it.

Over time, my curiosity turned to the technology around me. I wanted to understand how it worked, and then to build with it myself. That curiosity became the decision to study IT.

Today I see my two paths as one story. Each gave me something the other needed, and together they define how I work.

04 / Professional values

What I stand for at work.

01

Creativity

02

Professional kitchens run on routine, timing and standards. I bring the same consistency and attention to detail to my work in IT.

03

Adaptability

Moving between two industries has taught me to learn quickly, accept change and stay calm when things do not go to plan.

04

Problem-solving

Whether it is a failed sauce or a failed connection, I enjoy finding the cause, fixing it properly and learning from it.
